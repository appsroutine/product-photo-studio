Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · Applies to Product Photo Studio version 2.0 and later

1. Who we are

Product Photo Studio (the “App”) is published by Habibe Atik (“we”, “us”), Istanbul, Türkiye, who acts as the data controller for the limited information described in this policy. Contact: appsroutine@gmail.com.

2. Summary

The App is designed to process your photos on your device. We do not operate servers for the App, and the App is not designed to upload your photos to us. Your photos and projects are kept on your device and remain under your control. The App does not include advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.

3. How your photos are processed

  • Background removal and editing are performed on your device using Apple’s frameworks (such as Vision and Core Image).
  • You choose photos with the system photo picker, so the App only receives the photos you select. You can also take a photo with the camera inside the App.
  • Exported images are saved only where you choose (for example Photos or Files) or shared through the apps you pick. We do not receive copies of them.

4. Information stored on your device

  • Projects (photos, cutout masks and edit settings) are stored in the App’s private storage on your device. Deleting a project removes it from the App; deleting the App removes the App’s data from the device, except the Keychain entry described below.
  • App settings and the number of free exports used are stored on your device. The free-export count is also kept in the device Keychain so that it stays the same after a reinstall; this entry may remain on the device after the App is deleted.
  • If you use iCloud Backup or back up your device to a computer, iOS may include the App’s data in that backup. Backups are provided by Apple under your account, and we do not have access to them.

5. Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment card details, billing address or Apple Account password. The App checks your purchase status with Apple on your device. Apple’s privacy policy applies to payments: apple.com/legal/privacy.

6. Optional diagnostics

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may provide us with crash and performance information about the App, managed according to Apple’s privacy practices. This information is not intended to include your photos. You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

7. Contacting us

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the information you choose to share, and we use it to respond to your request. Emails are handled through our email service provider, which stores them on its servers in other countries. We keep support correspondence only as long as needed for support and reasonable record-keeping.

Please avoid sending sensitive personal information by email. You can ask us to access or delete your information, subject to applicable law.

8. Information we do not intend to collect

The App is not designed to collect your photos or exported images, contacts, location, browsing history, advertising identifiers or usage analytics, and we do not build advertising profiles. We do not sell or rent personal information.

9. Permissions

The App may ask for access to the camera (to take product photos) and for add-only access to your photo library (to save your results; this permission does not let the App read your library). You can change these permissions at any time in your device settings.

10. Legal bases

Where data-protection laws such as the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) or the EU/UK GDPR apply, we process support emails on the basis of our legitimate interest in answering you. Diagnostics are collected and shared by Apple based on the choice you make in your device settings.

11. Retention

  • On-device content: kept until you delete it or delete the App (see the Keychain note above).
  • Support emails: as long as needed for support and reasonable record-keeping, unless you ask us to delete them (subject to applicable law).

12. Security

Because processing takes place on your device, your content is protected by the security features of your device and iOS, such as device encryption and your passcode. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. Your rights

Depending on where you live and the applicable law, you may have the right to access, correct or request deletion of personal information you have shared with us, such as the content of support emails, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, contact appsroutine@gmail.com. You may also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority. Projects stay on your device, so you can delete them yourself at any time.

14. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has contacted us, let us know and we will delete the message.

15. Third-party services and links

The App relies on Apple services (such as the App Store, StoreKit and on-device frameworks) and may link to external pages. Those services and pages are governed by their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices.

16. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date shows the latest version. Continued use of the App after an update means the updated policy applies, to the extent permitted by law.

17. Governing law

This policy is governed by the laws of the Republic of Türkiye. This does not limit any rights you may have under mandatory data-protection law of your country of residence.
